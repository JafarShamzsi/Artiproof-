## 2. REVENUE MODEL — FREEMIUM + PAID TIERS

| Tier | Price | Who It's For | What They Get |
|------|-------|-------------|---------------|
| **Free** | $0 | Students & researchers | 10 checks/month, basic plagiarism + AI detection |
| **Pro** | $20/month | Faculty, independent researchers | 200 checks/month, citation-ring detection, integrity score, API access |
| **Institution** | Custom pricing (contact sales) | Universities | Unlimited checks, admin dashboard, LMS integration, analytics, priority support |

**Why $20/month Pro works:**
- GPTZero charges $15–24/month for 1 feature (AI detection only)
- Copyleaks charges $14–75/month for 2 features
- ArtiProof gives **5 features** for $20 — plagiarism, AI detection, citation rings, integrity score, and fact-check
- Best value on the market

**Why custom institutional pricing:**
- Turnitin, Copyleaks, and every major player uses custom quotes for universities
- Allows us to price based on university size, budget, and region
- Flexibility to offer aggressive pilots in Azerbaijan, higher rates in EU-associated EaP countries
